Primer Pair Check & In-silico PCR

Enter a forward and a reverse primer (5'→3'). The page checks whether they can stick to themselves or to each other at the 3' end (primer-dimers), whether they fold into hairpins, and whether their melting temperatures match. If you also paste a template DNA, it finds where the primers bind and shows the PCR product.
